# Page Lab formats

Source: ClickDose Labs "Page Lab" (the gallery of formats they build). Each format below is a live-page type; the right-hand column says which blueprint in `blueprints.md` to build it from.

Present this to the user as a menu (format → what it's for → fits this product?), then recommend one and a second choice.

| Format | What it's for | Traffic | Build from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepages / Brand Sites | The domain's front door: brand story, navigation, several products | Mixed, direct, search | Sales Page |
| Landing Pages | Generic campaign page; pick the format by traffic temperature | Any | Chooser in `blueprints.md` |
| Advertorials | Editorial story that sells before the reader feels sold to | Cold, readers | Story or Founder-Letter Advertorial |
| Listicles | "N things your ___ isn't doing": teach the mechanism in skimmable items | Cold, skimmers | Listicle |
| Five-Reasons Pages | Listicle with N=5, one belief per reason, offer at the end | Cold, skimmers, impulse price | Listicle |
| Pain Point Pages | One pain in the customer's own words, one page | Cold, problem-aware | Story Advertorial |
| Sales Pages | The full argument in one scroll | Warm, considered purchase | Sales Page |
| Offer Pages | Closer: price, bundles, guarantee, buy | Pre-sold | Offer Page |
| Entry Product Pages | Low-price first purchase, short page | Cold/warm, impulse | Offer Page |
| Second-Half Pages | The page after a presell; picks up where it ended | Pre-sold | Offer Page |
| Product Pages | Gallery, variants, details, reviews | Search, warm | Offer Page + proof |
| Product Launches | New product: what's new, date, preorder/waitlist | Warm, audience | Sales Page |
| Live Demo Pages | Interactive or video demo replaces the mechanism section | Solution-aware | Sales Page |
| Lead Gen | Form or call above the fold | High-intent services | Lead Gen Page |
| Quiz | Questions → personalised result → offer | Cold, curious | Lead Gen (quiz replaces form) + Offer result |
| Compare Pages | "Us vs them: an honest comparison" | Solution-aware searchers | Comparison Page |
| Switcher Pages | For users of a named competitor, with emphasis on how easy switching is | Competitor users | Comparison Page |
| Parody Product Pages | Humour format; still one CTA and a real offer | Social/viral | Any |

## Quick picks

- **Impulse-priced product (under ~$30) + cold social traffic:** Five-Reasons Page (1st), Pain Point Page (2nd)
- **Considered purchase + warm or retargeting traffic:** Sales Page
- **The ad caption already sells:** Offer Page or Entry Product Page
- **Service business, calls or forms:** Lead Gen (or Quiz)
- **Buyers comparing you with a named alternative:** Compare or Switcher Page
- **The bare domain / type-in traffic:** Homepage (Sales Page anatomy), linking to the campaign page

Tip: keep one format as the homepage and a second as the test page on its own path (e.g. `/` and `/journal`), so traffic can be split later.
